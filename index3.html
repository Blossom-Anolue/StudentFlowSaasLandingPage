FAQ

Quick answers.

Can I import my class schedule from my school? +

Yes — paste your syllabus or connect Google/Outlook calendar. We'll map classes, deadlines, and recurring sessions automatically.

How do reminders work? +

Smart reminders adapt to your study pace. Get a daily preview, plus nudges 24 and 2 hours before deadlines via email, push, or SMS on Pro.

Which calendars and tools integrate? +

Two-way sync with Google Calendar, Outlook, and iCal. One-click export to Notion, plus webhooks for power users.

Is there a student discount? +

Pro is already $4/mo for any student. Verified university unions can claim 50% off Campus plans through our partner program.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Cancel from settings — no calls, no friction. Your data exports cleanly as CSV or ICS.
