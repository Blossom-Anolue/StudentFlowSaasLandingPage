==============================================================

- Serious ↔ Playful:      3
- Reserved ↔ Expressive:  3
- Modern ↔ Timeless:      5

  "Track deadlines with ease"
  "Stay ahead of assignments"
  "Smarter scheduling for students"
  "One dashboard for student life"
